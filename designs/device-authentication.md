# Device Authentication

It's one of most important themes of current project because PasswordWand device has limited capabilities to enter information by human. There are only rotary encoder and buttons on it. No keyboard and easy way to type complext master password containing alphas, numeric and special symbols.

Yes, theoretically it's possible variant to attach USB/Bluetooth keyboard to PasswordWand but it's not reasonable and not usable if by design device itself should be enough. 

Another case - biometric usage - fingerprint or similar. But such variant is dangerous for User's health. At first, there are many films showing that needed parts of human body cut when needed to get access to restricted area with biometric lock protection. At second, cheap biometric sensors can be easialy hacked, more compex and robust has high price and it's not suitable for projct principe - "as cheapest device as possible to be available to anyone".

One more variant of inputs - voice recognition. "Tiny" AI models are exist for recognizing short commands. But entering password/text symbol by symbol using voice is also slow and somebody may hear you ...

Using rotary encoder and buttons is a compromise solution.
